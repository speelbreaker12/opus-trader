Contract Section: §2.2.2 - Policy Staleness Check

Requirement:
"PolicyGuard MUST check policy freshness on every tick.
If last_policy_update exceeds staleness_threshold, TradingMode
MUST resolve to ReduceOnly. The reject reason for blocked OPEN
orders MUST be RejectReasonCode::PolicyStale."

Guard: PolicyGuard.policy_stale()
Threshold: staleness_threshold (configurable)
Expected: ReduceOnly when stale, Active only when fresh
