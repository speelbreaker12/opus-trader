Contract Section: §2.3.8 - Pre-Trade Risk Check

Requirement:
"OPEN orders MUST be rejected when ANY of the following are true:
1. Net position would exceed max_position_usd
2. Daily loss exceeds max_daily_loss_usd
3. Open order count exceeds max_open_orders

Each condition MUST produce a distinct RejectReasonCode:
- PositionLimitBreached
- DailyLossLimitBreached
- TooManyOpenOrders"

Guard: PreTradeRiskGate
Conditions: 3 independent checks, each with its own reject reason
