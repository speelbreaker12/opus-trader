Contract Section: §2.3.5 - Liquidity Gate

Requirement:
"OPEN orders MUST be rejected when the L2 book snapshot is older than
l2_book_snapshot_max_age_ms. The reject reason MUST be
RejectReasonCode::BookSnapshotStale."

Guard: LiquidityGate
Trigger condition: L2 snapshot age > max_age_ms
Expected behavior: OPEN blocked with BookSnapshotStale
