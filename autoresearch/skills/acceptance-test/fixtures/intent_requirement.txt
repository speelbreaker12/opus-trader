Contract Section: §2.1.3 - Intent Classification

Requirement:
"UNKNOWN intent MUST be classified as OPEN (most restrictive).
Only intents with reduce_only=true MAY be classified as CLOSE.
Replace orders MUST be treated as cancel + new OPEN placement."

Classification rules:
- reduce_only=true -> CLOSE
- reduce_only=false or None -> OPEN
- replace order -> OPEN (new placement gates apply)
- unknown/missing -> OPEN (fail-closed)
